# X3Wars

X3Wars is a first-person vector rail-shooter for the RayNeo X3 Pro smart glasses, built in the spirit of the classic 1983 color-vector arcade cabinets: glowing wireframe ships and terrain on a transparent black background, with a young pilot and an old mystic voice guiding you through the fight. Three battles cycle endlessly with rising difficulty, mixing open-space dogfights, ground assaults, and tense trench runs that end in a single well-timed torpedo shot. The game runs on just two inputs, swipe to aim and tap to fire, with two fully voiced characters performing pre-generated dialogue throughout.

## Controls

- Swipe (4-way) — steer the aim reticle, or fly the ship during a run
- Tap — fire the cannons, launch torpedoes, start, or retry
